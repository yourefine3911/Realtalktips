5 Signs He Actually Likes You Over Text

If you're tired of being ghosted, read this. Most people miss sign #3.

1. He replies fast, even when he's busy

Not just "hey". He asks follow-up questions. He remembers what you said yesterday.

2. He texts you first in the morning

Good morning texts are not random. It means you were the first person on his mind.

3. He wants to move off the app

This is the biggest sign. When a guy wants to actually meet you, not just keep texting forever.

4. He makes plans, not excuses

"Let's meet this weekend" > "We should hang out sometime"

5. He is consistent

Not hot today, cold tomorrow. Consistency is attraction.
